That's the end of Topic 4. Here's the whole pie on one page, and one job for the next time money comes in.

## What we covered

- **Turn the formula around:** income minus saving and giving equals what you can spend.
- **Your income is a pie,** cut into three slices: Generosity, Future and Living. Every income, not just salary.
- **Generosity:** family, faith and community, given from a plan, not from the leftovers.
- **Black tax, planned:** a fixed amount on a fixed day, a small pot for requests, and big needs planned as goals. Your savings are not the family's emergency fund.
- **Future:** emergency fund, faster debt payoff, goals, protection and long-term saving, moved on payday.
- **Living:** what's left. Track it, keep room for fun, and if it doesn't fit, start with smaller slices instead of faking it.
- **Starting splits:** Seed 70/20/10, Grow 60/25/15, Harvest 50/30/20. Start where you are, move up when you can.
- **Grow the pie:** earn more, and decide where the extra goes before it arrives.
- **Lifestyle creep:** save at least half of every raise.
- **Traders:** sales aren't income. Separate the money, pay yourself a salary, set aside tax, and slice the salary.

!> Next time money lands, slice it first.

## Your job this week

On your next income, before you spend anything:

1. Write the amount.
2. Choose your split: Seed, Grow, Harvest or a smaller start.
3. Work out your three amounts in naira.
4. Move the Future and Generosity slices the same day.

If you use the Kopkad app, the Wealth Builder does the maths and shows each slice for you every time money comes in.
