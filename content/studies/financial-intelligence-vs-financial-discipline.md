Most of us already know the basics. Spend less than you earn. Save something. Avoid debt you can't repay.

So why don't we do it?

Because knowing isn't doing. Getting your money right takes two different things, and most people only work on one of them.

## Intelligence is the plan

Financial intelligence is understanding your money: where it comes from, where it goes, what you're aiming for, and the best route to get there. It answers questions like:

- How much do I actually spend in a month?
- What am I saving for, and by when?
- Is this debt helping me or hurting me?

Intelligence is what you work out when you sit down with a pen, a calculator and an honest mind.

## Discipline is the daily habit

Financial discipline is doing the plan when it's inconvenient. It is:

- choosing needs over wants at the moment you're tempted,
- keeping part of every income before you spend it,
- saying "not this month" to the outing, the upgrade or the aso-ebi you hadn't planned for.

Discipline isn't one big decision. It's dozens of small ones every week.

!> Intelligence without discipline is a plan that stays on paper. Discipline without intelligence is hard work in the wrong direction.

## Why you need both

Esther knows exactly what she should save each month. She's even written it down. But by the 20th the money has gone on small things she can't remember, so the plan never happens. She has the intelligence; she's missing the discipline.

Michael is very disciplined. He saves every week without fail, but he keeps it all in a current account where inflation quietly eats it, and he's never worked out what he's saving for. He has the discipline; he's missing the intelligence.

Neither of them is careless. Each has half of the skill.

## Make discipline easier

Discipline gets easier when you don't have to rely on willpower:

- **Save first, automatically.** Move your savings the day money lands, before you can spend it.
- **Decide in advance.** Choose your limits for the month before the month starts, not at the till.
- **Keep the goal in sight.** A goal with a number and a date ("₦300,000 by June") is easier to protect than "save more".

We'll build the plan, the intelligence part, over the coming weeks. Start practising the discipline part now.

**Think about it:** which is harder for you right now, making the plan or sticking to it?
