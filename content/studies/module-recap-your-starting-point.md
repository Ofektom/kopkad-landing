That's the end of Topic 2. Over three weeks we've gone deep into step 1 of the journey: knowing exactly where you stand. Here's all of it on one page.

## What comes in

- **Count every source:** personal, business, investment and other income. Most people's income is bigger than their salary.
- **Plan with take-home pay,** not the figure on your offer letter. Under the tax law in force since 1 January 2026, the first ₦800,000 of taxable income a year is tax-free, and pension, NHF and rent relief reduce what you're taxed on. The old consolidated relief allowance is gone.
- **If your income changes,** plan on your lowest month and save the good months.

## What goes out

- **Fixed expenses** come whether you like it or not. The lower they are, the safer you are.
- **Variable expenses** are where the control is: cut costs, cut back, cut out.
- **Watch the small leaks:** data, transfer fees, snacks, unplanned rides.

## What's left

- **Surplus = income − expenses.** Your household profit, and the raw material for every goal.
- **Savings rate = surplus ÷ income.** Aim for 20% or more over time, but start wherever you are.

## What you've built

- **Assets:** what you own at today's value. Know which ones pay you and which cost you.
- **Liabilities:** everything you owe, including loan apps, instalments, advances and family.
- **Net worth = assets − liabilities.** The real scoreboard, and more honest than any salary.
- **Cash flow isn't income:** a loan is cash in but not income; most of a repayment is cash out but not an expense.
- **The dam:** the level only rises if more flows in than out, month after month.

!> You can't improve what you don't measure.
