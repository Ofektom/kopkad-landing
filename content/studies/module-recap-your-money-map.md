That's the end of Topic 1. Here's the whole journey on one page.

## What we covered

- **Your salary is not your wealth.** Michael and Mercy earn the same, but after 100 days one has ₦10,000 and the other ₦100,000. Wealth is what you keep and own.
- **Intelligence and discipline.** Knowing what to do is the plan; doing it every day is the habit. You need both.
- **The six steps of the journey:**
  1. **Know where you are:** income, expenses, savings, what you own, what you owe.
  2. **Choose your destination:** goals with a number, a date and a reason.
  3. **Find the gap:** what the goal costs minus what you'll have if nothing changes.
  4. **Plan the route:** earn more, spend less, save regularly, plan for tax, invest wisely. Combine them.
  5. **Watch the risks:** job loss, illness, rising prices, scams, family emergencies. Build a buffer.
  6. **Walk it, together:** discipline, focus and grit, with the people you live with on the same page.

!> Six steps, one page. Everything else in this class builds on them.

## Your job this weekend

Put step 1 on paper. Get the free **Money Map** below. It's one page: money in, money out, your surplus, what you own, what you owe and your net worth, plus a quick safety check on how many months your savings would last. It takes about 20 minutes. If you prefer, the spreadsheet version does the maths for you.

Use your bank and mobile money history for the last month. Don't aim for perfect; aim for honest. A rough map today beats a perfect one you never start.

Then, on the back of the page, write your top goal and its monthly gap from step 3. Together, that's your starting point for everything that follows.
