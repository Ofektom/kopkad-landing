You've got your starting point, your goals, your gap and your route. Then one Tuesday the landlord raises the rent, or your mother falls ill, or your company cuts staff.

Step 5 of the journey is asking, before it happens: what could knock me off the road?

## The common risks

Most money plans are derailed by the same handful of things:

- **Losing your income:** a job ends, a contract isn't renewed, business slows down.
- **Illness or injury:** yours, or someone you're responsible for. Hospital bills arrive fast and rarely in small amounts.
- **Rising prices:** inflation quietly makes every goal more expensive and every naira you hold worth less.
- **Bad investments and scams:** money put somewhere that promised a lot and returned nothing.
- **Death or crisis in the family:** funerals, emergencies and the support that's expected of you.

!> A plan without a buffer works only until the first bad day.

## Three ways to protect yourself

**1. Build a buffer.** An emergency fund, money set aside only for genuine emergencies, is the first line of defence. Even a small one changes everything: a ₦50,000 cushion can be the difference between handling a bill and taking a loan at high interest. We cover how big it should be in a later topic.

**2. Don't put all your eggs in one basket.** One income, one customer or one investment is fragile. A second income stream, savings in more than one place, and investments spread across different things all reduce the damage any single shock can do.

**3. Learn the warning signs.** Many of the worst losses are avoidable: "guaranteed" returns, pressure to bring in others, no clear business behind the money. If an offer sounds too good to be true, it is.

## Plan for it, don't fear it

The point isn't to worry about everything that could go wrong. It's the opposite: once you've thought about the risks and built some protection, you can get on with your plan with less anxiety.

## Your turn

Which of these risks would hurt you most right now? Write it down, and one small thing you could do about it this month. For most people, that one thing is starting an emergency fund, however small.
