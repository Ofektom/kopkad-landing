Once you know your gap, the question is how to close it. When most people hear "save more", they think of one thing only: cut spending until it hurts. Then they give up after a month.

There are actually five routes, and the strongest plans use several at once.

## The five routes

1. **Earn more.** A side skill, extra hours, a small trade, asking for the raise you've earned. Cutting has a floor; earning more doesn't.
2. **Spend less.** Not by suffering, but by being deliberate. Cut the cost of what you buy (cheaper data plan, buying in bulk), cut back on how often (eating out twice a month, not twice a week), or cut out what you don't really value.
3. **Save regularly.** Small amounts, every payday, before you spend. Regular saving beats occasional big efforts because it builds the habit.
4. **Plan for tax.** Know what tax you pay and the reliefs you're entitled to under the rules in force since January 2026, such as pension contributions and rent relief. Money you don't overpay is money you keep.
5. **Invest wisely.** Once you have a cushion, put longer-term savings where they can grow faster than prices rise, and never into anything promising "guaranteed" high returns.

!> Most people only try one route. Combine two or three, and each one has less to do.

## Back to Esther

Esther needs to find ₦25,000 a month for her rent. Trying to cut ₦25,000 from her spending alone would be painful. So she combines routes:

- **Earn more:** she does weekend hair styling for two clients a month, about **₦12,000**.
- **Spend less:** she switches to a cheaper data plan and cooks lunch three days a week, about **₦9,000**.
- **Save regularly:** she moves her savings on payday instead of at month-end, so the "leftover" ₦4,000 she used to lose to small spending now goes to savings, about **₦4,000**.

₦12,000 + ₦9,000 + ₦4,000 = **₦25,000**. No single change is dramatic. Together they close the gap.

## Order matters

- **Start with saving regularly.** It's free and it changes everything else.
- **Then spend less:** look at your step 1 numbers for the easy wins.
- **Earn more** when you can; it's often the biggest lever over time.
- **Invest** only after you have an emergency cushion, which we cover in a later topic.
