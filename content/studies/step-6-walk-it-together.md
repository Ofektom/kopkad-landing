Plenty of people make a good money plan. Far fewer follow it for a whole year. Step 6 is where most journeys stall: actually walking the road.

## What it takes

**Discipline:** doing the plan on the days you don't feel like it. Saving on payday when there's something you'd rather buy. We covered this in the second study: knowing isn't doing.

**Focus:** keeping your eyes on your own goals. There will always be a new phone, a new trend, a friend's lifestyle on social media. Their journey isn't yours.

**Grit:** carrying on after a setback. You'll have a bad month. An emergency will eat your savings. Starting again is part of the plan, not a sign it failed.

!> A plan you restart after every setback beats a perfect plan you abandon.

## Bring your people with you

This is the part most money advice skips. Very few of us manage money alone. A spouse, a partner, parents, siblings: their decisions affect yours, and yours affect theirs.

A plan made in silence usually fails in silence. You save hard for the rent; your partner, not knowing, books a trip. You cut back on outings; a relative, not knowing, asks for help you'd already budgeted elsewhere.

Talking about money is uncomfortable for many of us. A few things make it easier:

- **Share the goal before the numbers.** "I want us to own our place in five years" opens a better conversation than "you spend too much".
- **Have a regular money talk.** Fifteen minutes once a month to look at where things stand. Calm, planned, not in the middle of an argument.
- **Agree on what each person handles.** Who pays what, what you save together, and what each of you can spend freely without asking.
- **Plan family support openly.** Decide together how much you can give each month, so generosity doesn't quietly empty the savings.

## Find a money partner

If you live alone, find someone to walk with: a friend with similar goals, a sibling, a savings group. Telling one person your goal makes you more likely to reach it.

## Your turn

Who do you talk to about money? If the honest answer is "no one", pick one person this week and share one goal with them.
