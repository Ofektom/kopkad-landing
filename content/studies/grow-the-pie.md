So far we've talked about how to cut the pie. There's a second way to get bigger slices: make the pie bigger.

## Two ways to grow it

**Personal income** is the money you earn from work:

- a promotion or a raise you prepare for and ask for,
- a skill that pays more, or a certificate your field values,
- side work: weekend jobs, tutoring, making and selling, freelancing,
- for traders, better prices, more customers or a second product line.

**Investment income** is the money your money earns: interest, dividends, rent. It starts small, and it grows as your Future slice grows. We cover it properly in Topic 18.

## Grace grows her pie

Grace starts tutoring two secondary school students on Saturdays and earns **₦50,000** a month extra. Her pie goes from ₦200,000 to ₦250,000.

On the Seed split, every slice grows:

- Living, 70%: ₦140,000 → **₦175,000**
- Future, 20%: ₦40,000 → **₦50,000**
- Generosity, 10%: ₦20,000 → **₦25,000**

But Grace was already living on ₦140,000. So she makes a better choice: she keeps Living where it was and puts most of the extra into her Future slice. Tomorrow's study explains why.

## Saving has a limit; earning doesn't

You can only cut your spending so far. There's no limit on how much you can earn over a lifetime. The best plans do both: they control the slices **and** grow the pie. Topic 16 is all about growing your income.

!> A bigger pie means bigger slices, but only if you decide where the extra goes before it arrives.
