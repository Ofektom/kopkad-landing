Medium-term goals sit between the urgent and the far away: things you want within one to five years.

## Typical medium-term goals

- capital to start or grow a business,
- a car or motorbike, especially one you'll earn with,
- a wedding,
- a deposit on land,
- a professional qualification or a master's degree,
- a fully built emergency fund of several months' expenses.

## The trap: today's price

The biggest mistake with medium-term goals is saving towards today's price. Over one to five years, prices rise.

Nigeria's official inflation rate was about **14.6%** in September 2026. That means something costing ₦1,000,000 today could cost around ₦1,150,000 in a year if prices keep rising at that pace. Over three or four years, the difference becomes large.

!> Price medium-term goals for the year you'll buy them, not the year you start saving.

## A rough way to allow for it

You don't need a financial calculator. For a rough figure, add about **15% for each year** until the goal, building on the year before:

- 1 year away: today's price × 1.15,
- 2 years away: × 1.15 × 1.15 (about 1.32),
- 3 years away: × 1.15 × 1.15 × 1.15 (about 1.52).

Prices might rise faster or slower than that. Check again each year and adjust.

## An example

Joy wants **₦1,500,000** of capital to open a provisions shop in **2 years**.

- Allowing for rising prices: ₦1,500,000 × 1.32 ≈ **₦1,984,000**.
- She adds a 10% buffer for things she hasn't thought of: about **₦2,182,000**.
- She's already saved **₦300,000**.
- What's left: about ₦1,882,000 over 24 months, or about **₦78,400 a month**.

Planning on ₦1,500,000 would have left her about ₦680,000 short on the day she was ready to start.

## Where to keep it

Money you won't need for a year or more can work a little harder than short-term savings, for example in fixed-term savings or other low-risk options that pay more interest. Medium-term money still shouldn't go anywhere it could suddenly fall in value just before you need it. We'll look at the options properly in the investing topics.
