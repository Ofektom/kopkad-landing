That's the end of Topic 3. Here's everything on one page, and one job for the new year.

## What we covered

- **Goals come before budgets.** A budget without a goal feels like punishment; a goal gives saving a purpose.
- **Values first.** Name your top three values and check that your spending matches them.
- **Short term (within a year):** rent, school fees, a starter emergency fund. Keep the money safe and easy to reach.
- **Medium term (1 to 5 years):** business capital, a car, a wedding. Price them for the year you'll buy them.
- **Long term (5+ years):** a home, your children's education, freedom from money worries. Start early, even small.
- **Life stages:** focus on today's stage, keep an eye on the next.
- **SMART goals:** specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound.
- **Pricing a goal:** today's price, plus rising prices, plus extras, plus a buffer, minus what you have, divided by the months.
- **Priorities:** safety buffer, then expensive debt, then the full emergency fund, then goals by date and importance, with long-term saving started early.

!> Write it down. Price it. Date it.
