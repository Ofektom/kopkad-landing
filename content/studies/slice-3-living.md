The third slice is the one most people start with. In the pie, it comes last. You live on what remains after your future and your giving have been taken care of.

## What goes in the Living slice

- **Fixed costs:** rent (saved monthly), school fees (saved monthly), minimum loan repayments, subscriptions.
- **Everyday costs:** food, transport, data and airtime, electricity, water, toiletries.
- **Personal and fun:** clothes, hair, eating out, outings.

## Grace's Living slice

Grace lives on **₦140,000** a month:

- Rent, saved monthly: **₦45,000**
- Food: **₦40,000**
- Transport: **₦20,000**
- Data and airtime: **₦7,000**
- Electricity and water: **₦8,000**
- Personal care: **₦10,000**
- Fun and eating out: **₦10,000**
- **Total: ₦140,000**

Everything has a place, including fun. A Living slice with no room for enjoyment won't last.

## Making it enough

- **Track it.** You can't stay within a number you don't watch. Topic 7 covers simple ways to track.
- **Cut the cost, cut back, cut out:** the three tools from Topic 2.
- **Contentment.** Living well within your slice is a skill. The goal isn't the most expensive version of everything, but a good life you can afford while your future grows.

!> Living is the residual. Spend what's left, not what's there.

## If your Living costs don't fit

Sometimes the honest answer is that rent and food alone take more than 70%. Don't fake it. Start with smaller slices, for example 5% to the future and 5% to giving, and raise them as you lower costs or earn more. A small slice you keep is worth more than a big one you abandon in week two.
